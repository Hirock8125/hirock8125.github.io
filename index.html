🧐 What is ulka?

ulka is an experimental browser agent — a smart helper that lives inside your Windows computer. It reads the content of any webpage and performs tasks for you automatically. Think of it like a robotic assistant that can surf the web, extract information, fill forms, click buttons, and navigate websites—all from a simple command.

Because it uses advanced AI engines (FX, Jev, and Vercel AI Gateway), ulka understands what you want. You just give it a goal like "check my email inbox" or "download all PDFs from this page"—and ulka does the rest. No coding. No complex setup.
